## Install

Install `air-gestures-{tag}.apk` on an Android 10+ phone.

Claim the band in the app (Meta sign-in on Meta's own page, band in pairing mode),
or import the key of a band claimed with the Linux app
([air-gestures](https://gitlab.com/896kb/air-gestures)) under Setup. Claiming makes
the phone the band's owner and unlinks it from Meta's glasses and app.

Tested on a Xiaomi 17 Pro. On HyperOS, turn on Autostart for the app and set its
battery saver to No restrictions, or the background service gets stopped.
